Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a commercial activity? +
No. The experience is offered as a gift. We ask for heart-centered donations to cover venue, logistics, and facilitation. There is no profit motive—only the intention to share this transformative experience.
What are the prerequisites? +
None. Only an open heart and open mind to receive. No meditation experience, spiritual background, or prior knowledge is required. Skeptics are welcome—the experience speaks for itself.
What languages are sessions in? +
Sessions are usually held in the local language of the location. English is available with a translator. What's important to know: the experience transcends language. The state of Ananda is felt, not spoken.
How do I know this is real? +
Read our testimonials. You'll find voices from newcomers, long-time practitioners, and even skeptics. The proof is in the experience—and in the consistency of what people report across diverse backgrounds and beliefs.
What should I expect? +
Experiences vary. Common reports include uncontrollable laughter, inexplicable joy, deep peace, a sense of unity with others, or profound stillness. Some people describe it as remembering something they'd always known. Others simply feel loved. Come with openness, without expectations.
Is there a refund policy? +
Since this is offered as a gift with donations, we trust your heart. If circumstances change, reach out and we'll work with you. The intention is always mutual respect and understanding.
